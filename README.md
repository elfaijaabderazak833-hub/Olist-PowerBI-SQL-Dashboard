# Olist PowerBI SQL Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview
This dashboard analyzes the Olist Brazilian E-commerce dataset from 2016 to 2018. 
The goal is to uncover key business insights about sales, customers, and product performance using Power BI.

**Tools Used:** Power BI, DAX, Data Cleaning, Data Visualization, SQL

## 📈 Key Insights
1.  **Geographic Concentration**: 70%+ of total sales come from `Sao Paulo`. Opportunity to expand to other states.
2.  **Strong Revenue Growth**: Total Revenue grew from `0.4M` to `1.2M` over the 2-year period.
3.  **Top Performing Categories**: `Health & Beauty` and `Watches & Gifts` are the highest revenue-generating categories.

## 📊 Dashboard Features
- **KPI Cards**: Total Sales: 1.36M, Total Orders: 9.893K, Total Customers: 10K, Avg Order Value: 162.69, Total Revenue: 1.61M
- **Revenue Trends**: Revenue by Year and Revenue Growth Over Time
- **Geographic Analysis**: Top 10 Customer Cities and Total Sales by Seller City
- **Product Analysis**: Top 10 Product Categories by Revenue
- **Payment Analysis**: Sales by Payment Type

## 🛠️ Data Cleaning Steps
1.  Translated `product_category_name` from Portuguese to English using `SWITCH` in DAX
2.  Standardized city and state names
3.  Created calculated columns for better readability
